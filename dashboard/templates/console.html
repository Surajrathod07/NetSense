Second year minor project · Information Technology

Intelligent Message Priority System for Critical Network Communication

A patient alarm and a routine handover can share a network connection. Port-based rules alone cannot tell which message needs attention first. NetSense reads the application message, estimates urgency and schedules waiting traffic before it enters TCP.

Hospital alarmsDisaster field reportsFactory failures

Python, Flask, scikit-learn, SQLite and TCP sockets power the five stages of interception, classification, prioritization, delivery and display.

Limitations and future scope

The synthetic English corpus supports an academic demonstration. Use validated alarm systems and trained operators for real emergencies. Sustained critical traffic can delay normal messages. Applications must use the NetSense adapter.

Proposed extensions include multilingual models, additional priority levels, DiffServ marking and human acknowledgement with escalation.
